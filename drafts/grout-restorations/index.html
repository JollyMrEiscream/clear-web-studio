How we brought back a travertine floor

This travertine floor was in very rough shape: fill popped out, pores packed with dirt and grime, and a dull, scratched finish.

  1. 1
    Deep clean. Orbital machine and a neutral-pH cleaner.
  2. 2
    Re-grout & fill. Re-grouted all traffic areas and filled the holes with epoxy stone filler.
  3. 3
    Hone. 200-grit diamond resin discs on a 17-inch honing machine, then 400, 800 and 1500 grit.
  4. 4
    Polish. Finished and polished with Diaglo and a hog's hair pad.
