01

Swarm recovery or cut-out?

Swarm recovery

A swarm is a cluster of bees that's resting on its way to a new home: on a branch, a fence or the side of a house. Dan recovers the swarm and takes the bees to a hive.

Cut-out

Cut-outs are when we have to open up a floor or wall or go under a home to recover the bees. Every recovery is different and requires various skills to remove the bees and comb. I'm a carpenter, and I have the tools and skills to remove the bees from your property.

We're beekeepers. We don't exterminate bees.
