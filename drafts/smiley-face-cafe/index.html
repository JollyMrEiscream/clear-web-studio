03

palmetto needed a spot like this

We started Smiley Face Cafe because Palmetto needed a spot like this! A place to grab REALLY GOOD coffee, a quick fresh bite, and just chill for a minute. After living and working in the food industry in Austin, we were spoiled with amazing options on every corner. We’ve been missing that so we decided to create it ourselves :) Now we’re here, serving up scratch-made eats, delicious drinks and a place for the community to hang. Whether you’re heading to work, wrangling kids, or just need a minute to yourself, we’ve got your back.
