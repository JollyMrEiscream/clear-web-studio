Proudly family owned

Kristen and James Balderrama both came up in restaurants, then spent years as mortgage processors, until a corporate downsizing sent them looking for something new. Searching online, they found Sweet Deli for sale. “We stumbled upon it,” Kristen says. They bought it in July 2023, rebuilt the menu with friends and neighbors as taste-testers, and kept the cookies, muffins and Danishes regulars loved.

We want to be known as the local, downtown deli.

Kristen Balderrama
