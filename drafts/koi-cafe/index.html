From the kitchen

A look at the plates and drinks that keep Venice coming back.

A family table in Venice

Koi Cafe is a family Vietnamese kitchen and boba stop — the kind of place where the broth is watched, the rolls are rolled by hand, and the coffee is strong.

Whether you are craving pho, a bowl of bún bò Huế, or a cold boba after errands on Pinebrook, we are glad you found us.

Find us on Facebook at koicafefla for specials and updates. Hours can shift — please call before you visit so we can make sure the kitchen is ready for you.
