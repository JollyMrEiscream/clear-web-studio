About us

Homestyle Vietnamese — and boba when you want something cold

From our Facebook family at koicafefla: we’re a small family kitchen. Most days it’s the classics. Sometimes we throw in fusion or untraditional surprises.

Guests come back for aromatic pho, spicy Bún Bò Huế, customizable bún bowls, fresh rolls with peanut sauce, and rice plates — including fusion favorites like Korean marinated beef and our house 7 Cơm G Roti.

Reviewers often mention the same thing: staff explain the sauces, help you wrap egg rolls in lettuce, and make the meal feel welcoming — not rushed.

  • Homestyle bowls Pho, Bún Bò Huế, and vermicelli — cooked the way we cook for family.
  • Fusion surprises Bao sliders, Korean marinated beef, roti plates — ask what’s on when you call.
  • Boba & coffee A great selection of boba teas plus strong Vietnamese coffee.
