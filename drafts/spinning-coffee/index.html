Side A · 01

Two best friends. One family farm. Really strong coffee.

Spinning Coffee is Vincent Truyen and Mohd Hasan, best friends and neighbors who wanted a small shop where people could slow down, listen to music, and talk. Vincent has a coffee farm in Vietnam, so the beans in your cup come straight from it.

Opened March 2026

We want a relaxing vibe … we want people to come here to listen to music and relax and talking to friends.

Vincent Truyen, co-owner (Bradenton Herald, Apr 2026)

Why the beans matter

Our Vietnamese iced coffee is condensed milk and very strong coffee, dripped slowly through a phin filter. Grocery-store beans don't come out right, which is why we bring ours in from Vietnam.
