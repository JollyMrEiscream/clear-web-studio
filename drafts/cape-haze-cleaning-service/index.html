Florida pro tips for a fresh, season-ready home

01

Flush & refresh the plumbing

Run water and flush unused toilets so the traps refill — it keeps bugs and sewer smells out.

02

Erase ant trails

Wipe counters and the pantry with a 50/50 vinegar mix and move dry goods into airtight containers.

03

Clear lanai & window tracks

Vacuum tracks, open the weep holes and trim bushes that touch the house.
