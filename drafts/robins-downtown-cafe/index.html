The menu, the short version

Breakfast all morning, lunch alongside it. Big plates, homemade, made to order.

Our omelets

Not your usual omelet. We cook a thin egg 'crepe,' fold it over several times and fill it with your choice of vegetables or meats, so it comes out light and fluffy. Try the Classic Greek 3-egg: sausage, tomato, onion, spinach and feta, with toast or a biscuit and home fries, hash browns or grits.

Pull up a chair on Old Main

Robin's is the kind of place where the staff knows the regulars by name. Grab a cozy table inside, or sit out on the sidewalk and watch downtown go by.

Saturdays, Oct–May

Farmers Market mornings. When the Bradenton Farmers Market sets up on Old Main Street, our outdoor tables are right in the middle of it. Come early.
